How a deal works

  1. Source

    We find a business with real trading history and a clear need for capital.

  2. Structure

    We set out the amount sought, the terms, the use of the money and the risks.

  3. Introduce

    We present the deal to investors and arrange a meeting with the founder. Investors take their time to decide.

Fulcrum does not hold or pool investor funds. Money goes directly into the venture an investor chooses.

All investments carry risk, including loss of capital. Nothing on this page is an offer to sell or a solicitation to buy any security, and nothing here is a promise or guarantee of any return. Investors should take independent legal, tax and financial advice before deciding.
